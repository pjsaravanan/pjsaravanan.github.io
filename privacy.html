Privacy Policy

Personal Drive Backup is used only by its owner, to store the owner's own backup files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses only the Google Drive account of the person who signs in (the owner).
  • It uploads, lists and deletes backup files it manages there. It does not read other content for any other purpose.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 20 October 2026.
